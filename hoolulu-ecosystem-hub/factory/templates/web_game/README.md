## What is in here

| --- | --- |
| `index.html` | markup — canvas, HUD, overlay, and the offer block |
| `style.css` | one theme, dark by default, light if your system prefers it |
| `app.js` | the whole game: loop, input, scoring, high score, share link |

No build step, no dependencies, no network requests. It plays offline.

## Make it yours

- Colours live in `:root` at the top of `style.css`.
- Board size and speed are `CELLS`, `BASE_SPEED` and `SPEEDUP` at the top of `app.js`.
- The offer block at the bottom of `index.html` is where the money is — point it
  at your own booking or licence page.
